How a season goes.

Three stages, in order — each one closes before the next opens.

  1. 1.0

    Register.

    Pseudo, email, password. Confirm and you’re in.

  2. 2.0

    Predict.

    Drag every club into the order you think it’ll finish, in each competition you want to play. Call the Champions League knockout ties too — full-time score, extra time included, and who wins the shoot-out if it’s still level. Lock it in before kickoff; it’s fixed after that.

  3. 3.0

    Score.

    Fifty points for a club in its exact spot, twenty for one place off, nothing further out — multiplied by how much of a surprise that position was against the bookmakers’ own odds.

Kickoff sets the deadline. Not us.
